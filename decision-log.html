Change Notices status model simplified: RFI is three states, PCN is two, approvals removed module-wide (D-043); Tasks tab change notices collapsed into one Change Notice Scopes section, CCN label dropped for PCN number (D-044)
D-043 Change Notices status model: RFI = Open / Awaiting Answer / Closed; PCN = Open / Closed; approvals removed entirely Committed
Source: Mike, Jun 17 (confirms the no-approval direction Justin set Jun 2) workflow ui-pattern data-model

Decision: Collapse the Change Notices status enums to the smallest set that reflects how Thor actually works. RFI runs Open (captured, not yet emailed), Awaiting Answer (PM has emailed the GC and is waiting), and Closed (the answer is captured or the question is moot). PCN runs Open (recorded on ingest, linked work in flight) and Closed (work complete, or the PCN is voided). Awaiting Answer renders amber so it reads distinct from blue Open; a voided PCN is just Closed with a reason.

Approvals removed module-wide. There is no internal approval gate and no Approved, Rejected, Pending Sign-off, or Draft status on either object. PCNs are inbound, GC-signed documents that Thor records rather than approves; RFIs post straight to Open whether raised by a field tech or the PM. Cost impact survives as a field, but the KPI is relabeled from "Approved Cost Impact" to "Cost Impact" since there is no approval to qualify it.

Reasoning: the earlier enums (Sent, Answered, Approved, Rejected, Pending Sign-off, Draft) modeled a compose-send-and-approve workflow that Thor does not run. Thor emails GCs outside Odin and trusts the field to raise RFIs that matter; Odin is the system of record, not an approval engine. Folding Sent into Awaiting Answer and Answered into Closed removes standing states that never carried independent meaning.

Affects: odin-change-notices-list.html, odin-change-notices.html, odin-change-notices-rfi.html, odin-change-notices-rfi-detail.html, odin-change-notices-pcn-detail.html; spec section 6.6 (tab intro, register, both state machines, RFI detail panel, mockup manifest). The voided-PCN detail render keeps a distinct red-tinted callout to flag the rare retraction case; this is informational, not an approval state.

D-044 Tasks tab: collapse per-PCN clusters into one "Change Notice Scopes" section; drop the Jonas "CCN" label for the PCN number Committed
Source: Mike, Jun 17 (from a Tasks tab screenshot review) ui-pattern workflow

Decision: The Tasks tab gives change notices a single Change Notice Scopes section instead of a separate detailed cluster for each PCN. The original per-PCN headers (approval date, cost impact, hours impact, reason, progress ring) read as too heavy and intimidating for this surface. The new section header is styled to match Original Scope: a combined hours rollup and a single est-vs-act bar, with a subtle amber left-border so the two scope sections read as a family. Per-PCN cost, date, and reason are not lost; they live in the Change Notices register and in the task detail panel.

Numbering: task rows now carry a PCN-XXX badge, the same identifier used when the PCN is ingested (tasks are created as PCN-004: [title]). The Jonas-internal "CCN" term that previously appeared as the badge and cluster label is no longer surfaced to users; it was confusing and added apparent complexity. The page subtitle and annotations were updated to match, and the stale "Approved · Fixed" cluster labels are gone (consistent with D-043, which removed approvals from the module).

Affects: odin-tasks.html (three PCN clusters merged to one; badges and subtitle relabeled). Spec section 6.5 (Change Notice Scopes cluster, Other-bucket placement, group toggle).

Tech mobile home redesigned and committed (Option A with Team Scoreboard, D-041); Work Orders to My Dept WO and Projects to Open/Closed, tech-to-WO assignment parked (D-042)
D-041 Tech mobile home redesigned and committed (Option A, with Team Scoreboard); resolves D-023 Committed
Source: Justin chose Option A (with scoreboard); KPI math was Mike's call per Justin's "just build it" ui-pattern mobile

Decision: Ship a redesigned tech mobile home: My Hours (personal, no estimate) at top, then a whole-company Team Scoreboard, then Work Orders and Projects. The scoreboard shows closed work only over a rolling 30 days, identical for every tech: Maintenance, Quoted Service Work, and Projects as over/under rings, plus an Emergency Service count compared to the previous 30 days. Tapping any work-order or project row expands a contributor panel adapted from the desktop hours-contribution hover.

Data rule (the crux): estimate-vs-actual is always measured at the work-order / project level, summed across everyone on the job, never per tech, because estimates live on the job and not per person. Every rollup and KPI uses group hours over grouped estimates. A tech's own hours appear only in the My Hours card and the per-job expand panel.

Color model: in-progress work uses a neutral blue budget gauge (no green; amber near the estimate, red once over before closing), because being under on an unfinished job only means it is not done. Closed work uses a green/red scorecard. Terminology: service-side "Projects" now reads "Quoted Service Work."

Reasoning: earlier mockups missed because they implied a tech could be measured against a whole job estimate alone. Anchoring all over/under math at the job level, keeping personal hours separate, and splitting open-vs-closed color semantics resolved it. Justin reviewed an A/B (with and without the scoreboard) and chose the scoreboard version.

Affects: odin-tech-home.html (replaced; project IDs deep-link to the per-project tech view, work-order numbers stubbed pending a service-side WO detail). Spec section 7.2 rewritten; phasing updated; D-023 marked resolved.

D-042 Work Orders tab is "My Dept WO" (not "My WOs"); Projects drops "My Projects" and defaults to Open/Closed Committed
Source: Shri and Mike, Jun 11 meeting ui-pattern mobile data-model

Decision: On the tech home, the Work Orders section leads with "My Dept WO" rather than "My WOs," keeping Open and Closed beside it. The Projects section drops the "My Projects" tab entirely and opens on Open, with Closed beside it.

Reasoning: Odin has no tech-to-work-order assignment. Jobs frequently carry several techs and that relationship is unsolved, so a personal "My WOs" cannot be computed honestly. Department, however, is attached to work orders and is already functional, so it is the working filter. Separately, techs are not attached to projects (sometimes assigned, often not), so a "My Projects" tab would show most techs an empty list. The real tech-to-WO relationship is a larger, separate piece of work, parked in spec open decisions item 12 so the rationale is not lost.

Affects: odin-tech-home.html. Spec section 7.2 and open decisions item 12.

D-038 RFI cluster: tech/foreman/supervisor can all raise; no approval gate; informal cost-impact notes; cost or hours change always becomes a PCN Committed
Source: Client review (Jun 10); Mike's calls on each sub-item workflow role ui-pattern

Who can raise an RFI: Technicians, Foremen, and Supervisors, any of the three, from the field. All use the same mobile composer (odin-tech-rfi-composer.html); the only difference is the created_by_role stamped on the record. Foreman and Supervisor composers each get a dedicated "Raise RFI" button.

No approval gate (reaffirmed, gate killed). The client review proposed adding a PM approval/screening step before an RFI goes to the GC. Mike killed it: Thor does not want an approval process. RFIs post straight to OPEN; the PM handles the outbound email and curates by acting, not gatekeeping. This also corrected stale "Submit creates a draft / PM reviews in a Draft Inbox" language still sitting in the RFI composer mockup, which contradicted the canonical state machine (6.6.5).

Potential cost impact = informal notes (reframed cost tab). The review asked for an "additional costs tab" on the RFI. Because Thor's rule is that any change to cost or hours is always formalized as a PCN, a formal cost ledger on the RFI would be redundant or contradictory. Chosen instead (Mike's call): an informal "Potential cost impact" notes section on the RFI detail where the field or PM can jot likely impact. The notes seed the PCN if one is raised; otherwise the RFI just closes.

RFI outcome model (confirmed). An RFI does not always become a PCN. A pure clarification is answered and closed (the existing Mark Answered / Close path, D-029). A change to cost or hours always becomes a PCN, via the "Create PCN from this RFI" action now shown beside Close on the Answered state, or via inbound PCN ingest with the Associated RFI set. The GC's response can arrive as an email (captured by the PM) or as a PCN; both paths are supported.

Foreman/Supervisor Outstanding RFIs: shown read-only, auto-pulled, and showing all open RFIs (not capped at three) so a foreman cannot miss one. The foreman never assembles this list by hand.

Affects: odin-tech-rfi-composer.html (de-drafted, generalized to all field roles), odin-change-notices-rfi-detail.html (informal cost-impact section both states; "Create PCN from this RFI" added to Answered), odin-foreman-report-composer-mobile.html and odin-supervisor-report-composer-mobile.html (all-open Outstanding RFIs + Raise RFI button). Spec 6.6.4, 6.6.5, 6.6.7, 7.13, 7.14 updated; `created_by_role` enum widened to ADMIN/TECH/FOREMAN/SUPERVISOR.

D-037 Add Tasks screen built; Jonas spreadsheet import is the bulk path Committed
Source: Client review (Jun 10): "Add Task" screen needed; explore Jonas to Excel to Odin import ui-pattern data-model

Decision: Build a dedicated Add Tasks screen as the destination for the Tasks tab "Add task" button (which previously went nowhere). It is a full-page admin form with repeating rows (one task per row: name, est. hours, optional due date), an "Add another task" affordance, a batch "Add to cluster" selector defaulting to Original Scope, auto-assigned Task IDs and a default Not Started status, and a sticky save bar with a live count. For long lists straight out of Jonas (30 to 75 tasks), Import from spreadsheet is the primary path; the manual screen is the small-batch and fix-up tool.

Import approach: a clean, structured Excel import with deterministic column mapping (Task name, Est. labour hours, optional cluster/due), not AI classification, so the seeded estimates can be trusted. Estimate accuracy is the point of the module, so AI is kept as an optional convenience layer rather than the thing integrity depends on. Open dependency on Thor: the Jonas export mixes materials and labour and we only want the labour rows for tasks. Pending request to Thor (via Justin) for either a labour-only export or a column that flags row type (Labour vs Material). The import UI is built once that file format is confirmed; the manual Add Tasks screen ships regardless.

Bulk add feasibility: 50 to 75 rows in one form and one bulk save is trivial on both client and server; no performance concern. The reason to prefer import is human error over many hand-keyed rows, not a technical limit.

Affects: new file odin-tasks-add.html; odin-tasks.html "Add task" button now links to it. Spec section 6.2.1 added; the former "bulk task import from templates" out-of-scope line replaced by the planned Jonas import path.

D-036 One time entry: project hours captured and allocated in the Daily Tech Log; timesheet becomes a read-only roll-up Committed
Source: Client review (Jun 10) asked for hours-to-task allocation on both the daily log and the timesheet; everyone flagged the two-systems confusion; Mike + Claude resolved the model, Mike endorsed data-model ui-pattern workflow

Decision: A tech enters their project hours exactly once, in that project's Daily Tech Log. The log carries one or more time blocks (each an optional time range plus an hours value; "add another time block" covers a return visit later the same day), and the day's total is then allocated to tasks in hours (not percentage) with a live "X of Y hrs allocated" counter; any unallocated remainder auto-lands in the project's Unallocated bucket (D-031). The standalone project timesheet stops being a second entry form and becomes a read-only roll-up of what was logged today, broken out by task. A tech who worked two projects in a day files one Daily Tech Log per project; the "two timesheets in a day" case is therefore just two project logs, not a special form.

Why: two screens collecting hours was the source of the confusion raised by every reviewer ("are these the same entry, two entries, or two places to type the same thing?"). The fix is to make hours one thing, not to coordinate two. Hours-based allocation (not percentage) is required because the Tasks tab tracks estimates in hours, so actuals must reconcile in hours; percentage would add mental math and rounding drift. Putting the entry in the Daily Tech Log matches the module's proof-of-work mission (hours are part of the record of the day) and is where the tech is already describing the work task by task. Allocation is encouraged but not blocking; the Unallocated remainder keeps the numbers honest without forcing the tech to over-attribute.

Open integration detail: how the project-log time writes into the existing Odin (service-side) timesheet so the tech does not re-enter hours anywhere. Owned by Mike + Mandeep, not a blocker for the mocks. Also captured as a Thor training point (techs must understand project time does not also go in the regular timesheet).

Affects: odin-tech-log-composer.html (Hours stepper replaced by Time blocks; "Tasks worked on" replaced by hours-per-task allocation with a live counter; Unallocated row now shows the auto remainder), odin-tech-time-submit.html (reframed from entry form to read-only "Project time today" roll-up; entry point now opens the log). Spec sections 7.5 and 7.12/7.12a updated; the per-project "Submit Time" button rerouted to the composer.

D-035 Dictation strategy: native mic button in v1; record-and-transcribe and AI clean-up deferred to v1.1 Committed
Source: Client review (Jun 10) asked for dictate + AI-assist on mobile; Mike set the phasing ui-pattern scope

Decision: Add a visible microphone button to the long-form text fields on the mobile composers (daily tech log narrative, RFI description, foreman/supervisor report notes) for v1. The v1 implementation uses the browser-native speech recognition engine (Web Speech API): the field's own mic, on-device, no server round-trip and no per-use cost. Two follow-ons go to the roadmap, not v1: (1) record-and-transcribe (server-side, Whisper-type transcription for more consistent quality), prioritized in v1.1; (2) an AI "clean up" button that polishes dictated text, scheduled right after 1.0.

Reasoning: a visible mic button genuinely drives dictation use (the same reason Claude and ChatGPT surface one), and techs strongly prefer dictating over typing on site, so it earns its place in v1. But it costs almost nothing to ship because the browser already provides the engine: the mockup change is just the icon. The clean-up button is different in kind, a real AI feature with per-call cost and plumbing, and Mike wants 1.0 shipped before it is taken on so it does not become a side-quest that pulls the devs off the launch. Record-and-transcribe is the quality upgrade path once the basics ship.

Affects: mockups gain a mic affordance on long-form fields (implementation in the next build batch): odin-tech-log-composer.html, odin-tech-rfi-composer.html, odin-foreman-report-composer-mobile.html, odin-supervisor-report-composer-mobile.html. Spec section 13: native mic in v1, record-and-transcribe and AI clean-up in v1.1.

D-033 Client / GC portal is a post-launch addition, not part of v1 Committed
Source: Justin asked (email, Jun 10); Mike confirmed the deferral and Justin aligned scope integration

Decision: External login access for clients and general contractors (a "client portal" to check project status) is NOT in scope for this build and will be done as a separate addition after the module launches, on a time-and-materials basis. The v1 external mechanism stays as committed: internal users generate on-demand project report PDFs from the Client Reports tab and email them out. No external logins in v1.

Why it's not already in scope: the signed SOW (Jan 5, 2026) is explicit. Section 1 and section 4 rule out external stakeholder dashboards/portal access; section 3.6 commits to controlled external visibility through reviewed PDF reports "rather than granting ODIN access"; section 8 lists external logins/portal as exactly the kind of thing requiring a change request. Mike's quoting email to Justin matched this ("control what is shown to them through reports, not allow them to login directly into Odin"). So both sides agreed to the no-portal posture in writing from the start.

Why defer it rather than fold it into this build: two reasons, both agreed with Justin. (1) Keep the team focused on shipping the committed scope cleanly. (2) Let the module accumulate real project data and let Thor's staff get familiar with it before exposing it to a customer, so a client's first login isn't to a freshly-launched module that's sparse or out of date. The portal shell already exists on the service side, so the future work is the project-specific functionality, not the portal itself. Justin is aligned on doing it post-launch.

Resolves: the "Send to Portal" affordance flagged on the Change Notices detail (pending Brent) should not assume a portal exists in v1. It is either cut or reframed to the report/PDF mechanism. Confirm with Brent, but the SOW points to no portal in this phase.

Affects: spec section 12 item 6 (upgraded from open question to confirmed post-launch addition); items 2, 3, and 8 fold into the portal work when it happens. No mockup changes; this is a scope boundary, not a UI change.

D-031 Persistent Unallocated task bucket, created by default on every project Committed
Source: Christine (Thor co-owner), via Mike ui-pattern data-model

Decision: Every project is created with a permanent "Unallocated" task bucket. On the admin Tasks tab it renders as a default cluster pinned at the bottom of the list, with neutral grey treatment (distinct from amber PCN clusters), no hours estimate, hours-logged only. In the mobile daily tech log composer, a matching "Unallocated" row is pinned at the bottom of "Tasks worked on," rendered quiet (italic, muted, dashed divider above) so it is not the easy default pick. Time logged without a task attribution (timesheet Unallocated pick or the composer's Unallocated row) lands in this bucket; the PM can reassign those entries to real tasks later.

Reasoning: Christine's specific ask. Techs often do work that does not map to a defined task, or do not know which task applies. A persistent Unallocated bucket gives that time a real home rather than leaving it unattributed, while keeping it deliberately at the bottom and visually quiet so it is a fallback, not the default. It also gives the PM a clean queue of "time that needs a task" to reconcile. Counts toward project logged-to-date but never toward an est-vs-act calculation, since it has no estimate.

Also resolved here: the "what if there are 40 tasks" question on the composer. The "Tasks worked on" list gains a search box with the tech's in-progress tasks listed first, so it scales; the Unallocated row stays pinned at the bottom regardless of search.

Affects: odin-tasks.html (default Unallocated cluster), odin-tech-log-composer.html (search box + pinned Unallocated row). Spec section 6.2 (creation default), section 7.5 (composer), and Task entity (`is_unallocated_bucket`) updated.

D-029 RFI response capture defined as a modal; whole-RFI Download removed Committed
Source: Mike, reviewing the RFI detail mockup ui-pattern admin-side

Decision: Clicking "Mark Answered" on an Open RFI opens a "Capture GC response" modal with three fields: a response-text paste box, "Response from" (sender name + org), and "Date received". On save, the RFI flips to ANSWERED and the pasted text renders in the purple Response box attributed to sender and timestamp. Separately, the whole-RFI "Download" button is removed from both states of the detail panel; per-attachment download icons stay.

Reasoning: The mockup showed "Mark Answered" and the resulting purple response box, but never the actual capture step, so there was no way to enter the GC's reply. A modal (matching the PCN/RFI intake modal pattern) makes the capture explicit and keeps the response as a deliberate PM action rather than an auto-pull. The Download button was undefined beyond a tooltip and conflicted with the "PM handles email outside Odin" model (D-017); removing it keeps the panel lean.
