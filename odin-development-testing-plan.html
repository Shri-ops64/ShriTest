PLAN Testing plan — five phases
Testing happens in overlapping phases, not as one block after development finishes. Phases 1 and 2 run during development. Phase 3 begins only once all development, including the developer's own integration pass, is complete.
1
Individual Screen Testing
~1 week, parallel
Page loading, fields/buttons/filters, validations, CRUD, permissions, responsiveness, empty/error states, spec conformance, and integration with Work Orders, TimeTracker, and related modules — tested as each screen lands.
2
Feature & Integration Testing
Overlaps dev, weeks 2–4
Whole feature areas once their screens are ready: creating/editing a project, task & subtask creation, time roll-up, status updates, roadblocks, reports, and admin/tech data consistency.
3
End-to-End Workflow Testing
~2 weeks, after dev complete
Full project lifecycle: creation, setup, tasks, technician updates, time entry & roll-up, status/progress, roadblocks, reports, closure, and historical review.
4
Defect Resolution & Regression
Overlapping, inside Phase 3
Logging defects with repro steps, developer fixes, retesting, regression on related screens, and confirming existing ODIN functionality isn't affected.
5
Final Review & UAT Readiness
Final 1–2 days of Phase 3
Confirm screens complete, critical defects resolved, workflows validated, data consistent, permissions checked, and the module stable enough for client UAT.

If the missing 2 days are real, every downstream date in this plan is 2 days optimistic. Worth resolving directly with Mandeep rather than absorbing silently.
