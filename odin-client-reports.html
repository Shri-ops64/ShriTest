Client Reports tab notes

  • Three vertical zones: Generate (top), Pending Approval (middle), Report History (bottom). Reads top-to-bottom as a workflow.
  • Generate panel is compact: 3 dropdowns (period, template, tone) and one big Generate Draft button. Below the form, a meta strip shows what data the AI will draw from and how long generation takes. The AI tag (purple) flags this as an AI-generated action.
  • Pending Approval queue shows two states: "Awaiting Admin Review" (amber, fresh AI draft) and "Draft (Not Submitted)" (grey, admin started editing but hasn't submitted yet). Each row has type-appropriate actions: Review & Approve for fresh drafts, Edit + Submit for in-progress drafts.
  • Pending summary preview is the first paragraph of the AI-generated report, so the admin can eyeball whether it captured the period correctly before opening the full editor.
  • Report History table shows what's been sent: title, reporting period, status (all "Sent" in this view), sent timestamp, recipient, approved-by. Actions: view, download, resend.
  • Right column gives context that frames the workflow: stats (counts, last sent, cadence), Scheduled Reports (Bi-weekly auto-generation that drops into Pending Approval), Recipients, and the workflow visualization (Generate → Review → Send, current step highlighted blue).
  • The workflow visualization is intentional onboarding. New admins immediately understand that AI doesn't auto-send. Admin approval is the safety gate.
  • Scheduled Reports is the auto-pilot feature: setting it up means a fresh draft lands in Pending Approval every 2 weeks (or whatever cadence), so the admin's job becomes review-and-send instead of generate-from-scratch.
  • Approval gate is non-bypassable in v1. Every report goes through human review before client sees it. AI is a drafting assistant, not an autopilot.

What sits behind the "Review & Approve" button

  • Opens the full report editor (separate screen). Admin can edit sections, regenerate individual paragraphs ("regenerate this section with more detail on PCNs"), tweak tone, add/remove content. Once happy, click Approve & Send. Email goes out with PDF attached.
  • The output design of the actual report sent to client is defined by odin_client_report_mockup.html (the previously-produced reference).
