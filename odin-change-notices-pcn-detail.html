PCN detail panel notes

  • Three states on one page for review purposes. In production, this is one panel that renders different content based on the PCN's status and billing type. State 1 (Fixed, Open) is the most common case; State 2 (T&M, Open) is the new June 2 variant; State 3 (Voided/Closed) is rare but needed for the edge case where a GC retracts a previously-signed PCN.
  • Billing type pill in the header. Per June 2 direction: PCNs come in two flavors. The pill renders next to the status pill to make the billing type immediately visible. Fixed = grey pill (standard); T&M = amber pill (alert color, since the dollar amount is not fixed at intake and accrues via hours logged).
  • Signed PCN document is the source of truth. Treated visually as the most important element on the page: prominent card with red PDF icon, signer name, signed date, and inline View / Download actions. The metadata fields below are convenience indexes around the document.
  • Header carries the RFI back-reference badge when the PCN was triggered by an RFI (the most common path per the directional model). Clicking jumps to the source RFI. In this example, State 1's "From RFI-007" is shown because PCN-005 came back from the RFI Adam raised on May 28.

State 1: Open · Fixed billing (the most common case)

  • Impact shown as two big tiles: Cost impact, Hours impact. Cost is green when $0 (no billable change) to subtly highlight the "no cost" case is intentional, not missing data.
  • Linked tasks section is the dynamic heart of the panel. Lists each task spawned from this PCN with its current status, est-vs-act bar, and hours used vs estimate. Updates as work progresses; PM can come back to this view a month later to see how the PCN's work is tracking.
  • "Add another task to this PCN" affordance below the list. Used when work emerges during execution that wasn't in the original intake composer. Spawned tasks are still tagged with this PCN's pcn_id so they appear in the right Tasks tab cluster.
  • Action row in the header: Close PCN (primary, when work is complete), Edit (for metadata only; signed doc never changes), Export PDF, Archive, Void. Void is the secondary danger action.

State 2: Open · Time & Materials billing (added June 2)

  • No cost or hours estimate fields. Per Justin's June 2 lock: T&M PCNs don't carry upfront budget values in Odin. The two big tile values from the Fixed state are replaced with a "Time & Materials" impact block showing hours accrued so far and the last-logged entry.
  • No revenue calculations. Odin does not multiply hours by labor rates. No dollar values appear anywhere on a T&M PCN inside Odin. Hours data exports to Jonas; Andrea computes the billable revenue on the accounting side.
  • Why this restraint matters. Building dollar conversions in Odin would require storing labor rates per employee (or per role), handling overtime multipliers, handling different rates for billable vs non-billable hours, etc. That's an accounting feature, not Odin's scope. The June 2 framing keeps Odin firmly in the "operational" lane.
  • Justin can still derive sales analytics from the hours data: "which techs are generating the most hours through T&M PCNs that came from RFIs", "average hours per T&M PCN on this project", etc. The raw hours field is enough for those questions.
  • Spawned tasks carry no estimate. The bar shows total accrued hours, not progress against a budget. PM marks the task Complete when GC indicates work is done; PM marks the PCN Closed when ready to export hours to Jonas for invoicing.
  • Linked tasks helper text below the task list reminds the PM that T&M tasks lack an estimate, and walks through the close-out flow (task Complete first, then PCN Closed when hours are ready to export). Reduces the chance of accidentally closing a T&M PCN before all hours are logged.
  • Audit trail captures every logged entry, not just status transitions. T&M PCNs need a clean record of who worked, when, and how many hours, because that record IS the invoice basis.

State 3: Closed (PCN voided after ingest, rare)

  • Void reason captured prominently in a red-tinted box at the top, with the name and timestamp of who voided it. The reason is required when an admin clicks Void; PM has to explain what happened (GC retraction, owner-side change, dispute, etc.).
  • Signed document remains visible but dimmed, with a "Retained for record" pill. Critical for audit purposes.
  • Original impact values shown with strikethrough. Reader immediately sees "this WAS $850 / 6 hrs, but no longer applies."
  • Linked tasks were auto-cancelled when the PCN was voided. Banner explains that any hours already logged are retained on timesheets for audit but the tasks themselves are closed out as Cancelled status.
  • No Edit action available on a voided PCN. Locked from further changes.
  • Voiding works the same for Fixed and T&M PCNs. The state 3 example shows a Fixed PCN being voided (with a dollar amount struck through); a T&M PCN being voided would have any logged hours retained on timesheets and the spawned task marked Cancelled.

Data model additions for PCN detail support

  • PCN-specific fields: pcn_billing_type enum (FIXED, TIME_AND_MATERIALS) (new June 2); gc_pcn_number (the GC's reference); gc_signed_by (signer name from the document); gc_signed_date (vs. ingested_at); gc_contact_id (foreign key to project's external_contacts entry).
  • For Fixed PCNs: cost_impact (decimal), hours_impact (decimal). Nullable for T&M PCNs.
  • For T&M PCNs: no estimate fields. Hours accrue via the spawned task(s) linked by pcn_id. Computed values like "hours_logged_so_far" are derived from time entries, not stored.
  • Voidion: voided_at, voided_by_user_id, void_reason (required text when status moves to Voided).
  • Task entity: CANCELLED status used when a parent PCN is voided. Tasks under T&M PCNs have estimated_hours = NULL.
  • Activity event types: PCN_INGESTED (metadata includes billing_type), PCN_VOIDED (metadata includes void_reason), PCN_CLOSED, TASK_CANCELLED.

Out of scope for v1

  • Labor rate storage and dollar calculations on T&M PCNs (Andrea computes this in Jonas)
  • Margin tracking or profitability analytics per PCN
  • PCN versioning / amendments (if the GC sends a revised PCN, Thor ingests it as a new PCN with a back-reference, rather than amending in place)
  • Bulk export of all PCN documents for a project as a single archive
  • External GC visibility of how Thor's tasks under their PCN are progressing (parked for v2 client portal)
