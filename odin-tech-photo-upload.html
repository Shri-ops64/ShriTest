Photo upload confirmation notes

  • This is Step 2. Step 1 is the native camera/library picker (handled by OS, not designed here). Step 2 is this confirmation screen, where the tech picks destination and optionally captions.
  • Lands here from: per-project "Add Photos" button, daily log composer's "From Library" button, My Photos screen's "Add" button.
  • Photo preview grid shows the selected photos with remove (X) buttons. Last-chance review before upload.
  • Destination radio is the key UX moment here. Two options:
    • Site Photos folder (default): photos go straight to the project's Site Photos folder, no log attachment
    • Attach to today's daily log: appears when the tech has a draft (or auto-creates one). Photos go to the draft as well as to Site Photos.
  • Default to Site Photos folder matches the original problem you raised: tech wants to upload without going through a log. Hard sell to make the daily-log attachment the default because that adds friction.
  • Optional caption applies to all photos in this batch. Tech can edit individual captions after upload via the photo full-size viewer (not mocked, simple modal).
  • Single big upload button at bottom, count in the label ("Upload 3 Photos") so the tech knows what they're committing to.
