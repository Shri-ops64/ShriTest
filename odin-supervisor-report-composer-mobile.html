Supervisor Report composer (mobile · v1)

  • Weekly cadence, after a site visit. Supervisor visits multiple projects each week; submits one report per project per visit.
  • Different lens than the foreman report. Foreman answers "what's happening on the project"; Supervisor answers "how well is Thor running this project." Per Justin's framing.
  • Health is the supervisor's own assessment, independent of the foreman's. When they disagree (Foreman green, Supervisor yellow), that's a useful signal for the PM.
  • Foreman performance is a single 🟢🟡🔴 (Strong / Adequate / Needs help), NOT a 6-category scorecard with letter grade. Optional one-line note. Per Justin scope conversation.
  • Verification of foreman report is the audit hook: Yes / Mostly / No. Discrepancies captured as free text. Surfaces gaps between what the foreman reported and what the supervisor observed.
  • Thor Standards is three categories in v1: Workmanship, Site cleanliness, Productivity. Each is G / Y / R. v2 expands to the full 6 Thor Standards categories.
  • Top 3 risks as three separate free-text fields. Forces the supervisor to actually pick three rather than dumping every concern into one big text box.
  • Support requested is the action hook: what does the foreman / PM need from leadership? Drives concrete follow-up.

What's NOT in v1 (parked, per scope conversation with Justin)

  • Foreman performance multi-category scorecard with letter grade (6 categories with 1-10 scores).
  • Full 6-category Thor Standards Audit (Tool organization, Professional appearance, Customer experience, Material organization; these 4 parked).
  • 6-category Safety Audit with individual scores (PPE, Ladder, Lift, Fall protection, Hazard ID, Housekeeping). v1 captures safety as free text.
  • Labour productivity audit with specific waste categories (waiting for material, rework, etc.).
  • Coaching opportunities longitudinal tracking. (HR territory; needs careful design before building.)
  • AI-generated executive summary, biggest risk/opportunity, cross-project trends, "projects requiring executive attention."
  • Overall Job Score (___/100). v1 health rating is the supervisor's own assessment.
  • Cross-project company-wide aggregation dashboard for Justin (the most exciting v2 feature, but its own roadmap conversation).

What feeds into rollup elsewhere in Odin

  • Supervisor health rating alongside foreman health on Overview tab. Two signals, surfaced for the PM to interpret. Odin does not auto-merge into one score.
  • Verification flag (Mostly / No) generates an Activity feed event for the PM.
  • Top 3 risks are stored as free text in v1. v2 could promote them to first-class Risk records with owners and follow-up dates.
  • Foreman performance trend visible on the Project Logs tab over time (rolling green/yellow/red across weeks). No HR-style file in v1.
