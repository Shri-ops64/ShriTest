My Tasks (full list) notes

  • Lands here from the "View all" link on the per-project view's My Open Tasks section.
  • Scoped to "my tasks on this project". Tech sees their own assigned tasks only, not the full project task list (that's admin-side).
  • Filter chips: Open (default, shows Not Started + In Progress + Blocked), All, Complete. Counts shown.
  • Grouped by status in priority order: Blocked first (most urgent for the tech), then In Progress, then Not Started. Complete tasks shown only when the Complete filter is active.
  • Blocked tasks get a red left border on the card plus the inline blocking-reason banner with the "Blocked since" date. The reason text comes from the admin-side `blocked_reason` field.
  • Status icons: hollow circle = Not Started, blue dot = In Progress, red with line = Blocked, green check = Complete.
  • Due dates highlight when soon (amber, "Due May 22 (2 days)") or overdue (red).
  • Hours logged shown when In Progress to give the tech a sense of cumulative work.
  • Tap a task opens the Task detail screen (next mockup) where the tech can change status, view full description, etc.
