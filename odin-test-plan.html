How this saves and shares now: every status change, note, dev response, and screenshot saves straight to a shared Supabase database the moment you make it — there's no export/import step anymore. Anyone on the team who opens this same page (on the deployed site) sees the same data. Changes made elsewhere show up here automatically every 20 seconds, or immediately if you click Refresh.

The "Tester" field at the top just labels your own changes with a name so the team can see who reported or resolved what — it's remembered in your browser only, not sent anywhere except attached to your saves.

The green/red dot next to "Connecting…" shows whether this page can currently reach the database. If it turns red, your changes are not being saved — check your connection and click Refresh. If you're viewing this file locally rather than on the deployed site, the API isn't available at all and this will always show offline — that's expected.
