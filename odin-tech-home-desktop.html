Section 7.1 · Tech Home (KPI scoreboard)

Desktop conversionv1

Converted from mobile mockup, "Projects" section removed per this request Built from the mobile Tech Home (v3, KPI scoreboard) dashboard. Kept: Hero, Quick Actions, My Hours, Team Scoreboard, Work Orders. Removed entirely: the standalone "Projects" card (Open/Closed tabs, rollup banners, project rows) — techs reach projects through the top-nav Projects tab now, so this duplicate list wasn't needed here.
My Hours is now clickable The whole card is a click target (hover shows a lift + blue border, and a chevron next to "your logged time" signals it) that opens the tech's detailed hours view — e.g. Daily/Weekly Timesheets. The period toggle (This Week / Month / YTD / All Time) still switches independently inside the card via event.stopPropagation(), so choosing a period doesn't also trigger navigation.
Layout: hero full-width, two-column row below Quick Actions and My Hours sit side by side under the hero (mobile stacks everything vertically). Team Scoreboard and Work Orders stay full-width since both need the horizontal room — the KPI rings run in a single row of 4 instead of a 2×2 grid, and Work Orders rows lay out ID/client/type on the left with the estimate bar on the right in one line instead of stacking.
My Hours — personal, no estimate (unchanged) Your own logged time for the selected period. Billable / Non-billable / OT breakdown and client count carried over unchanged.
Team Scoreboard — closed work, rolling 30 days (unchanged) Maintenance and Quoted Service Work rings show hours logged vs. estimated on closed work only — a finished job is the only time you know the real number. Green = under, red = over. After Hours and Emergency Service each show a call count with a trend delta instead of an estimate, since there's no estimate to compare against for either.
Work Orders — three tabs, all-hands hours (unchanged) My Dept WO / Open / Closed. Hours shown are the whole job's hours from everyone who worked it, against the job's estimate — not just the individual tech's time, since an estimate covers the whole crew. Open jobs get a neutral progress bar; closed jobs get green/red based on how they landed. Techs aren't tied to individual jobs yet, so each tab shows the full department/open/closed set with a "Show all" link into the full list.
Team Scoreboard update: Projects ring swapped for After Hours The "Projects" ring is removed from Team Scoreboard (Projects has its own home in the top nav now, and the est-vs-actual framing didn't add much there). In its place: After Hours, styled like Emergency Service — a plain call count in the center of the ring with a trend delta below, no estimate comparison, since after-hours work isn't quoted against an estimate either.
Click any row to expand (unchanged) Reveals who worked on it and their hours, including OT, plus the estimate line for reference — same interaction as mobile, just wider.
New: Billable, Non-bil, OT, and Clients are clickable Each of the three breakdown tiles and the Clients count now opens a descriptive modal — a hover state on each signals this. Billable/Non-bil/OT show which jobs make up that number; Clients lists each client worked for in the period with hours per client. All open on top of Home rather than navigating away, since these are "explain this number" moments, not destinations.
