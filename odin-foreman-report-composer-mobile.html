Foreman Report composer (mobile · v1)

  • Weekly cadence. Foreman submits this report once per week, typically end of day Friday or end of last shift on the project that week.
  • Pre-filled where possible. Project context, foreman name, week dates, and outstanding RFIs auto-populate from the project record and the RFI module. Foreman doesn't re-type known information.
  • Health is a single 🟢🟡🔴 traffic light (no scorecard, no scoring categories). Foreman picks one based on what they see. Optional one-line reason.
  • This week / next week as free-text blocks. Matches the structure of the current Kairos foreman report. Bullets in the textarea are accepted style; no fancy editor.
  • Subtrade traffic lights match the 7 subtrades from the existing Kairos report (Ventilation, Controls, Insulation, Firestopping, Air balance, Electrical, Sprinkler). Each gets G / Y / R / N/A.
  • Outstanding RFIs are pulled from the RFI module, not re-entered. Read-only display with age in days. RFIs that age past 7 days render in amber.
  • Two 1-10 ratings: Housekeeping and Project confidence. Slider control; no scorecard math behind it.
  • Photos use the existing photo-capture pattern shared with daily logs and RFI composer.

What's NOT in v1 (parked, per scope conversation with Justin)

  • Per-category scorecards (8 rated categories with 1-10 scores). Parked for v2 once we have baseline data.
  • Automated job-health score computation. v1 health rating is the foreman's own assessment.
  • AI section (executive summary, top 3 risks/opportunities generated, "if you were PM what would you focus on next week"). Parked for v2.
  • Change order log inside the report (already lives on Change Notices tab; cross-link instead).
  • Financial risk tracking inside the report (out of scope; lives in Jonas).
  • Detailed labour-per-employee productivity ratings. Time tab already captures hours; aggregate is enough at the report level.

What feeds into rollup elsewhere in Odin

  • Health rating → Overview tab "Project Health" indicator (manual assessment, foreman's voice).
  • Schedule status → Activity feed event when status changes from prior week.
  • Subtrade status → Activity feed when a subtrade moves to Red.
  • Confidence rating trend → Project Logs tab can show the rolling 1-10 line over the last 8-12 weeks (separate widget, defer to spec for placement).
