Mobile RFI composer notes

  • Five fields only, matching the lean intake spec (section 6.6.3). Photo upload, subject, description, linked task. Number and date auto-populated on submit, not asked. No recipient, no due date, no priority, no discipline, no drawing reference, no spec section, no blocking-task toggle. Field user captures, PM handles the rest.
  • Shared by all field roles (D-038). The same composer is used by Technicians, Foremen, and Supervisors. The only difference is the created_by_role stamped on the record; the form is identical. Entry points sit on each role's screen (see below).
  • Photo-first layout. Photo capture card sits above the text fields because the most common field-originated RFI starts with "look at this." Camera button is the primary affordance (dark filled), gallery picker is secondary (dashed border).
  • Two example photos rendered as illustrative SVGs: one showing a labeled SPEC CONFLICT shot of a mechanical installation, one showing a spec sheet with a highlighted line. Matches the photo treatment on daily logs and tech home for visual consistency.
  • Intro card sets expectation: "Submit posts this RFI to the project. The PM takes it from there and emails the GC. There is no approval step." Removes the anxiety of "am I being too casual" or "am I sending this to the GC directly", without implying a gate that does not exist.
  • Stage pill in the app bar reads "Internal capture" with a small info icon. Reinforces that this is the casual capture step, not the formal outbound send. Blue color matches the RFI type pill used elsewhere.
  • Subject hint helps frame the input: "one line" in the label hint, "Short. What's the question in five to ten words." in the help-note below. Keeps the field user from writing essays in this field.
  • Description hint reinforces casual tone: "In your own words. The PM will rewrite this for the GC if needed." The field user doesn't have to think about formal language; that's the PM's job when emailing the GC.
  • 500-character counter on description. Generous but bounded. Encourages capture rather than novel.
  • Linked task is optional with helper text "Skip if not sure." Tapping it opens a project-task picker (same pattern as the task picker elsewhere). Pre-fills the PM's mental model of "this blocks XYZ work."
  • Submit button uses the same pink-red gradient as other submit-style actions in the tech mobile app. Consistent affordance language.

What happens on Submit

  • Record is created as OPEN (D-038, no approval gate). The RFI posts straight to the project's Change Notices register as an Open RFI. There is no DRAFT state and no PM Draft Inbox; that earlier approval-style flow is cut (it conflicted with how Thor actually works, confirmed Jun 10).
  • Confirmation toast: "RFI raised. The PM will email the GC and follow up if anything needs clarification." Then returns to the per-project view.
  • The PM handles the outbound email to the GC from their normal email client, and later captures the GC's response back into the desktop RFI detail (Mark Answered). Odin is the system of record for the question and the answer, not the mail client.
  • Activity event emitted: RFI_CREATED with metadata noting created_by_role (tech, foreman, or supervisor). Shows up in the project's Activity feed so the PM and team see the new RFI as it comes in.

Entry points to this composer

  • Technician: "Raise RFI" affordance on the per-project tech view (odin-tech-project.html), below the two primary actions (Submit Time, Write Daily Tech Log).
  • Foreman: "Raise RFI" button on the Foreman report composer (odin-foreman-report-composer-mobile.html).
  • Supervisor: "Raise RFI" button on the Supervisor report composer (odin-supervisor-report-composer-mobile.html).
  • Possible future entry point from a task detail in case a field user wants to RFI directly against a specific blocked task. Parked for v1.1.

What field roles still do NOT do

  • They do not create PCNs. PCNs are inbound GC documents per the directional model; if a field user receives a PCN on site, they forward the email to the PM (per Justin's May 28 confirmation).
  • They do not compose-and-send RFIs. The outbound email to the GC is the PM's job. Mobile is intake only.
  • They do not answer RFIs. The PM captures the GC's email response into the desktop detail panel.
  • This composer plus the per-role Raise-RFI buttons are the entire mobile Change Notices surface for v1. Read-only register on mobile is parked for v1.1.
