Change Notices register notes

  • This is the dedicated list view with no modal overlay. Same register as on odin-change-notices.html and odin-change-notices-rfi.html, but here the table is the focus and the example rows demonstrate every key state.
  • PCN inbound, RFI outbound is the directional model locked in per Justin's May 28 confirmation. See spec section 6.6 for the full model and lifecycle.
  • Default sort: open items first (Open RFI → Awaiting Answer RFI → Open PCN), then by raised date descending. Closed items at the bottom.
  • Filter chips: All / PCN / RFI, then separately Open / Awaiting Answer / Closed.

The ten example rows demonstrate all the new states

  • RFI-008 (Open): PM created on desktop. Awaiting Compose & Send. The Compose action icon is the secondary affordance in the Actions cell.
  • RFI-007 (Awaiting Answer, due soon): Formal PDF was sent to the GC; amber Due date because the deadline is within 3 days. The Mark Answered icon is the secondary affordance.
  • RFI-006 (Awaiting Answer, overdue): Same state as above but Due date renders red.
  • PCN-005 (Open, linked tasks in progress, triggered by RFI): The most information-dense row. Shows the "From RFI-007" triggered indicator, the paperclip for the signed PDF, and the "5 tasks" indicator showing how many tasks are linked to this PCN. Hours show +2.5h.
  • PCN-004 (Closed): A recorded change, now closed. Document attachment retained for the record.
  • RFI-005 (Closed → PCN-003): Outcome badge in amber points to the PCN that came out of it. Clickable.
  • PCN-003 (Closed, linked back to RFI-005): Reciprocal of the row above. Shows the chain RFI → PCN clearly.
  • RFI-004 (Closed → Task): Outcome badge in green shows the answer triggered a single task without a formal PCN. Used when the GC's answer creates a small bit of additional work the client is absorbing.
  • PCN-002 (Closed, no RFI origin): A PCN that came directly from the GC without a Thor RFI preceding it. No "From RFI-XXX" indicator. Common case for owner-initiated changes.
  • RFI-003 (Closed → No action): Neutral grey badge. The most common closed-RFI outcome: question got an answer, no further work needed.

Column treatment refresh

  • Title cell can now carry three inline indicators: triggered-from badge (blue, PCNs only, when source RFI exists), outcome badge (closed RFIs only, points to what the answer triggered), paperclip with attachment count, and task indicator (PCNs only, shows linked-task count).
  • Status pills: RFI uses Open / Awaiting Answer / Closed. PCN uses Open / Closed. Approvals are not tracked in the module, so there are no approved or rejected states.
  • Due column applies only to Awaiting Answer RFIs. Other rows show a dash. Amber within 3 days of deadline; red when overdue.
  • Cost and Hours columns apply only to PCNs. Cost shows green text. Both open and closed PCNs carry their cost and hours.

Summary strip metrics

  • Total / Open / Cost Impact are the contractually meaningful numbers; these would also surface on the project Overview tab and in client reports.
  • RFI-to-PCN conversion rate is new. Shows how often Thor's RFIs result in a billable change order. Useful for the PM to understand whether the project is on a "creep" trajectory or whether most clarifications are absorbed.
  • Avg RFI turnaround from sent to answered. Measures GC responsiveness, useful for project health diagnostics.

Default row interaction

  • Click anywhere on a row opens the detail panel: PCN rows open odin-change-notices-pcn-detail.html, RFI rows open odin-change-notices-rfi-detail.html.
  • Actions cell affordances vary by state: Open RFI shows Compose icon; Awaiting Answer RFI shows Mark Answered icon; everything has Open / Download / Archive baseline.
